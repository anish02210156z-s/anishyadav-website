DMCA Copyright Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Legal Disclaimer: This document is intended as a general website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being relied upon as legal advice.

1. DMCA Compliance Statement

Anish Yadav respects the intellectual property rights of others and complies with the provisions of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). We respond expeditiously to notices of alleged copyright infringement.

2. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a copyright infringement notice, please provide our Designated Agent with a written communication containing:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and its specific URL location.
  • Your address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.

3. Counter-Notification Procedure

If your content was removed due to a DMCA notice and you believe it was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice containing your details, statement under penalty of perjury, and consent to jurisdiction.
