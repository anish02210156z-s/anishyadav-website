Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Legal Disclaimer: This document is intended as a general website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being relied upon as legal advice.

1. Principle of Data Minimization

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect the minimal personal information necessary to respond to contact form submissions, process newsletter subscriptions, or manage comment feedback.

2. Secure Storage & Encryption

All contact form messages and user interactions are encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption. Data stored in secure databases is protected by multi-factor authentication and restricted access keys.

3. Access Control & Retention

Only authorized site administrators have access to form submissions. We do not sell, rent, or lease visitor personal data to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

4. Contact Officer

For data access requests or deletion inquiries, email: anish02210156z@gmail.com
