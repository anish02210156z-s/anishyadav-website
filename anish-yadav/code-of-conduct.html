Code of Conduct

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Legal Disclaimer: This document is intended as a general website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being relied upon as legal advice.

1. Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to make participation in our open source repositories and website discussions a harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of background, skill level, or identity.

2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

  • Using welcoming and inclusive language.
  • Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
  • Focusing on what is best for the student developer community.

3. Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.
