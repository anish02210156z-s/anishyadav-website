Community Guidelines

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Legal Disclaimer: This document is intended as a general website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being relied upon as legal advice.

1. Community Vision

We aim to foster an inclusive, supportive, and educational environment for student developers, tech enthusiasts, and artificial intelligence researchers.

2. Expected Behavior

  • Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive in all discussions.
  • Share feedback politely and help fellow learners with code bugs.
  • Credit original code authors and sources.

3. Strictly Prohibited Behavior

The following are zero-tolerance violations:

  • Hate Speech & Harassment: Any form of discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or background.
  • Spam & Scams: Unsolicited promotional links, affiliate spam, or phishing attempts.
  • Impersonation: Pretending to be Anish Yadav or another contributor.
  • Malware & Exploits: Posting links to malicious payloads or pirated software.

4. Moderation & Enforcement

Comments violating these guidelines will be deleted without warning, and repeat offenders may be banned from participating in blog discussions.
