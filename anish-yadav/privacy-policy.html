Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 • Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Legal Disclaimer: This document is intended as a general website policy and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before being relied upon as legal advice.

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Anish Yadav (https://anishyadav.com.np). I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting the personal information you share with me. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, processed, and safeguarded when you access or interact with my personal portfolio, tech blog, educational articles, and web tools.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information in two main ways: information you voluntarily provide, and automated technical data.

A. Voluntarily Provided Data

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, and message contents when submitting a contact form or inquiry.
  • Newsletter Subscription: Your email address when subscribing to receive tech updates, blog posts, and educational guides.
  • Community Comments: Any public feedback, suggestions, or comments posted on blog entries.

B. Automated Technical Data

  • Server Logs: IP address, browser type, operating system, HTTP referrers, and timestamps automatically logged for security and operational monitoring.
  • Hosting & CDN Data: Request metrics processed via Cloudflare and GitHub Pages to ensure high performance and defend against DDoS attacks.

3. How Information Is Used

Your information is used strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To reply to your questions, project inquiries, or feedback.
  • To send requested email newsletters or educational resources.
  • To analyze website usage, debug technical errors, and optimize site performance.
  • To protect against malicious activity, spam, and unauthorized server access.

4. Third-Party Services

This website relies on trusted third-party service providers. Each third-party handles data under its own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare: Provides DNS routing, SSL encryption, and security filtering.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts public static web assets and server infrastructure.
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome: CDN delivery of typography and UI icons.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies and local storage exclusively to save your UI preferences (e.g., Light/Dark mode state). We do not deploy invasive advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. For more information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our site. Data submitted via contact forms is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill your request or maintain communication.

7. Children's Privacy

This website provides educational technology content and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your personal contact data.
  • Unsubscribe from email newsletters at any time.
  • Disable browser cookies via your browser settings.

9. Contact & Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
